About the System

A safe space, built for the CvSU Naic community

GAD: VPA is the official digital platform of the Gender and Development Office at Cavite State University Naic Campus. It exists to make safety, equality, and support easier to reach — for every student, faculty member, and staff.

The platform brings awareness, education, and reporting together in one trusted space, so that every voice is heard and every concern is acted on with care.

  • Promotes Gender and Development initiatives campus-wide
  • Raises awareness on violence prevention and response
  • Provides accessible educational resources and modules
  • Supports secure and confidential incident reporting
  • Encourages a safe, inclusive, and respectful campus

What the Portal Offers

Built around four core pillars

Every part of GAD: VPA is designed to make safety and support simple to access, for the whole CvSU Naic community.

Violence Prevention Awareness

Campaigns, advisories, and campus-wide resources to help everyone recognize, prevent, and respond to gender-based violence.

Incident Reporting

A secure, confidential channel for submitting and tracking incident reports, with guided support throughout the process.

Educational Resources

Multimedia educational videos, informative articles, and downloadable learning brochures that promote gender equality, healthy relationships, violence prevention, and rights-based protection.

Community Support

Direct access to counseling referrals, peer support networks, and GAD Office staff for guidance whenever it's needed.
